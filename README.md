# CalcHub- https://calchub-9k8.pages.dev/

CalcHub is an ed tech platform with 50+ calculators and utilities that are designed to help students, teachers, educators, athletes, financial analysts, and for personal use. It comes with detailed explanations, an FAQ section, and it saves your history locally and your favorites. This platform has no ads, is free to use, and is student-built.
This repo is to view the source code to prove that there's no tracking device, there are no tracking technologies, so your data is completely private and safe. If you would like to add this to your platform as a student tool, as an educational tool, or as any type of tool, please contact me. darkershadow10000@gmail.com. I built this educational technology tool over the summer, starting on June 28, 2026. I built this because I saw a calculator.net. It was pretty basic. The UI was pretty outdated. It was old, clunky, and filled with ads so I built an alternative that beats all of those. Called CalcHub it is free, has no ads, and does not check your data. All of your data is private. It's kept to yourself. Modern UI and is constantly updated. It's pretty fast. Has detailed explanations and an FAQ section below the calculator. It saves your past calculations and the past calculators you use, using local storage. And if you have a calculator that you really, really love, you can also favorite it! I built this platform on a budget phone, the Vortex J24 phone, and on a school Chromebook but now I shifted all of my operations onto my phone. I just want to thank you for reading this and I hope you have a great day. And remember CalcHub has every calculator you will ever need!
