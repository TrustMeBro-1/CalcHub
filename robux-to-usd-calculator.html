Quick Answers About Robux & DevEx

🤔 What is Robux?

Robux is the money in Roblox games. You can buy Robux with real money, then use it to buy game passes, cool outfits, pets, and other stuff in games. Think of it like coins in an arcade!

💵 How much does 1 Robux cost?

When you're buying: About $0.0125 per Robux (so 1,000 Robux = about $12.50)
When you cash out: You get less! About $0.0038 per Robux at the normal rate, or $0.0054 if you're 18+ and verified in the US. Think of it like selling something second-hand — you don't get back what you paid.

❓ Why does cashing out pay way less?

Roblox keeps some money to run the platform, keep servers safe, and pay other creators. It's just how it works. The buy price and sell price are always different — kind of like trading cards. You might pay $10 for a card, but when you sell it to a shop, they might only give you $5.

🎮 What is DevEx?

DevEx = Developer Exchange. It's how Roblox creators get paid real money for the Robux that players earn in their games. You can only use DevEx for Robux you earned from making games, NOT for Robux you bought yourself.

⚠️ What do I need to know about cashing out?

  • 🔢 Minimum: You need at least 30,000 earned Robux
  • 🎂 Age: You must be at least 13 years old (or have a parent set it up for you)
  • ✅ Verification: Your account and payment info need to be verified
  • 🌍 Location: You need to be in an allowed country (check Roblox for the full list)

💸 What's the 30% Roblox Fee?

When you sell something in Roblox (like a game pass), Roblox takes 30% as their cut. So if you earn 1,000 Robux from sales, Roblox keeps 300 and you keep 700 before cashing out. Use the "Apply Fee" toggle above to see how this affects your numbers.

👤 What's the 18+ US Rate?

Roblox pays a higher rate ($0.0054 instead of $0.0038) to creators who are 18+ and verified in the United States. It's a bonus if you qualify! You need to prove your age and be located in the US to get this rate.

❌ Is this calculator official?

No. This is just an estimate based on Roblox's published rates. Roblox can change prices and rates whenever they want. You can only buy Robux in fixed bundles (like $4.99 for 400 Robux), not in exact amounts. Always check Roblox.com for current prices before buying or cashing out!
